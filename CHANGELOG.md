# Changelog

## 2026-09-23

First public release.

- Platform scores for 91 platforms, JSON and CSV.
- Custody failure registry, including the live Coldcard entropy incident record.
- Hardware wallet rubric with per-score rationale.
- Custody model definitions with fit and failure.
- Features category re-scoped to custody-relevant capability only. It previously rewarded product catalogue breadth, which could award points for resembling the publisher. Scores recompute under the new definition at the October 2026 run.
- Coldcard loss figures updated to Galaxy Research's 24 August tally (1,789.28 BTC across 8,865 addresses) and the 21 September white-hat recovery of 52.37 BTC recorded, with the block checked on-chain.
